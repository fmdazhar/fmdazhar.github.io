Hey there! 👋  
I'm a **Robotics Engineer🤖** (M.Sc. Robotic Systems Engineering, RWTH Aachen), passionate and focused on **deep learning**, **reinforcement learning**, and **optimal control** to build intelligent, agile robots.

Currently I’m exploring learning-based control for manipulation and locomotion, building in **PyTorch**/**Jax**, **Python**/**C++**, **ROS1/ROS2**, **Docker**, and simulation stacks like **MuJoCo** and **NVIDIA Isaac Sim/Gym**.
